My Journey

This is my journey of learning computer networking from the basics and building my understanding step by step.

Before starting this journey, I knew very little about networking and how computers and other devices communicate with each other.

My curiosity about networking grew during my attachment, where I encountered people who were working with networking technologies and had a deeper understanding of things I did not yet know.

I often heard conversations about IP addresses, network configurations, Wi-Fi, and Packet Tracer simulations. I wanted to understand what they were talking about instead of feeling left out.

So I decided to start learning networking from the basics and gradually build my knowledge through both theory and practical work.
